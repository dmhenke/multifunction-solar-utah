# Multifunction Solar on Utah's Public Waters and Working Lands

A research-first strategy for solarizing public water bodies and working lands where solar has more than one function: power generation paired with water conservation, dust suppression, or ecosystem enhancement.

## Sites

### Little Dell Reservoir (drinking water)
- **Capacity:** 20,500 acre-feet, ≈249 surface acres
- **Current use:** Culinary supply, flood control, wildlife habitat
- **Strategy:** Demonstration (5% coverage, 5 MW) + scaling (25% coverage, 25 MW)
- **Key benefit:** Distributed generation near load; water-quality intervention via algal suppression; best-instrumented FPV research site in the Intermountain West
- **Challenge:** Culinary source means zero contamination risk is non-negotiable

### Great Salt Lake (sovereign lands)
- **Stage:** ≈4,191.6 ft (6.4 ft below 4,198 ft healthy target)
- **Strategy:** Playa ground-mount primary (1,605 acres, 402 MW_dc, 743,000 MWh/yr) + research FPV module (5 acres, 2 MW_dc)
- **Key benefit:** Material generation (≈83,000 households); dust suppression on exposed lakebed; avian impact research  
- **Governing principle:** No infrastructure that opposes lake recovery; all siting above 4,200 ft contour (lake recovery reserve)
- **Challenge:** Open-water FPV defeated by hydrodynamics, materials, ecology, and public trust

## Phased Approach

**Phase 0 — Research & Credibility (Months 0–3)**
- Literature synthesis
- University partnership
- Screening model validation

**Phase 1 — Policy Insertion & Instrumentation (Months 3–12)**
- Comment on CMP update (time-critical)
- Instrument existing projects (Signal Hill)
- Publish workshop synthesis

**Phase 2 — Funded Demonstration (Months 12–30)**
- Build small pilot at non-culinary water surface
- Agrivoltaics demonstration on working farm
- Publish results including negative findings

**Phase 3 — Structure the Venture (Months 24–42)**
- Advisory entity, research institute, or development company
- Convert Phase 2 credibility into operational entity

**Phase 4 — Scale to Named Sites (Year 3+)**
- Little Dell: approach with materials/water-quality evidence package
- Great Salt Lake: approach only with evidence thresholds met

## Key Insight

**Agrivoltaics on Utah farmland is a more powerful Great Salt Lake water intervention than floating solar on the Great Salt Lake itself.**

- Shading irrigated crops reduces crop ET 20–50% in arid climates
- That is a reduction in *consumptive* use — water legally dedicable instream
- Utah has a functioning legal channel: water-leasing and change-application framework

The strongest thesis: not "cover a lake" but "what is the marginal cost of a saved acre-foot and delivered MWh, jointly, across the portfolio?"
